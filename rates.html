A held day is closed to everything else, and inside a day it cannot be sold to anyone. Same reason for the four-hour remote minimum, which is the only minimum on this page.

Testimony is never capped. The schedule belongs to the court, and an examiner who has promised a not-to-exceed has a reason to want the day over.

It is billed to the retaining party whatever the outcome, and it is whatever the completed work supports. Where the opposing party notices the deposition, FRCP 26(b)(4)(E) ordinarily puts the fee on them. Where a matter requires a previously qualified testifying expert, that is stated in the engagement letter; I bring one in or decline.

Conditions

No discounts, promotions or volume tiers. Two things lower your bill, both published in advance: the standby retained rate, and the bank-transfer discount on engagement.

No minimum hours on examination or collection. Ten per drive and six per phone are published elsewhere in this field; a minimum that turns a two-hour job into a ten-hour invoice reads badly when the invoice becomes an exhibit.

Nothing starts before conflicts clear and the letter is signed. Money paid earlier is held unapplied and refunded in full, nothing deducted for card processing.

Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a licence, that is stated in the engagement letter and satisfied before work begins. I check your venue before I quote.

I will not

  1. Take a fee that depends on what I find, on the outcome, or on which side retains me.
  2. Take or pay a referral fee, in either direction.
  3. Take a vendor commission, or sell you the remediation I was paid to investigate.
  4. Bill an hour above the ceiling you authorised in writing.
  5. Image a whole disk where a targeted collection answers the question.
  6. Keep quiet about culling that would take your bill from five figures to four.
  7. Carry an overage or surge rate.
  8. Deduct card fees from money I am returning to you.
