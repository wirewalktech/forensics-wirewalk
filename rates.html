Quoted, not published

Any examination or analysis is hourly against a written ceiling. Server, RAID, NAS or virtual host imaging is a ceiling quoted after a technical call. Mobile physical, before-first-unlock, chip-off and ISP are quoted after inspection or referred out. A Rule 26(a)(2)(B) report is an estimated band with a stop-and-confer point. Intrusion response is staged from the mobilization block; breach notification lists are priced on record count.

Conditions

No discounts, promotions or volume tiers. Two things lower it: the standby retained rate and the bank-transfer discount on engagement.

No minimum hours on examination or collection.

Nothing starts before conflicts clear and the letter is signed; money paid earlier is held unapplied, refunded in full.

Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a license, the engagement letter states it and it is satisfied before work begins. I check your venue first.

I will not

  1. Take a fee that depends on what I find or which side retains me.
  2. Take or pay a referral fee, in either direction.
  3. Take a vendor commission, or sell you the remediation I investigated.
  4. Bill an hour above the ceiling you authorized.
  5. Image a whole disk where a targeted collection answers the question.
  6. Keep quiet about culling that cuts your bill from five figures to four.
  7. Carry an overage or surge rate.
  8. Deduct card fees from money I return to you.
