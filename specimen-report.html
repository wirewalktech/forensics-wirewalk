Method applied

[SPECIMEN — NOT A REAL MATTER]

The published procedure at /method, in the version current at the engagement date, which forms part of this report: ISO/IEC 27037:2012 principles, NIST SP 800-86 phases. Guidance I work to, not accreditation I hold.

A served report names each tool and version, the steps in order with timestamps, the artefact classes parsed, and the hypotheses tested and discarded — recorded as the work happens, because FRCP 26(a)(2)(B)(ii) reaches facts and data considered, not only relied upon. Write blocking was used on the laptop and cannot be used on a cloud source; there, soundness rests on documenting the export, hashing at the earliest point, and stating what could not be preserved.

Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a licence, that condition is stated in the engagement letter and satisfied before work begins.

Confidence words, with fixed meanings

[SPECIMEN — NOT A REAL MATTER]

Observed — readable in the artefacts, artefact identified. Supported — inferred from two or more independent artefact classes that agree. Consistent with — not contradicted, not established; other explanations named. Not established — the material does not answer it. The fourth is used as freely as the first three.

Limitations of the examination

[SPECIMEN — NOT A REAL MATTER]

Limitations

  1. Timestamps are as recorded by the source system. Where artefacts disagree, the disagreement is reported, not reconciled silently.
  2. The examination reaches what was collected; the manifest lists what was deliberately not.
  3. Nothing predates the earliest surviving log entry, and read receipts were unavailable at that licence tier.
  4. The removable device was not produced.
  5. No attribution to a person. A device is not a person, and an opinion on who was at the keyboard needs a separately stated methodology.
  6. Recovery of deleted data depends on medium, filesystem and encryption. Failed attempts are recorded, not omitted.
  7. If further material is produced, or an assumption proves wrong, the findings may change and I will say so in writing.

The published statement of what digital evidence cannot establish is at /limits, written before any engagement.
