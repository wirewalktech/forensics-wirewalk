Method applied

[SPECIMEN — NOT A REAL MATTER]

The published procedure at /method, in the version current at the engagement date: ISO/IEC 27037:2012 principles, NIST SP 800-86 phases. Guidance I work to, not accreditation I hold.

The served version names each tool and version, the steps in order with timestamps, and the hypotheses discarded, because FRCP 26(a)(2)(B)(ii) reaches data considered, not only relied upon. Write blocking cannot be used on a cloud source; there, soundness rests on documenting the export, hashing early, and stating what could not be preserved.

Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a licence, that is stated in the engagement letter and satisfied before work begins.

Confidence words, fixed meanings

[SPECIMEN — NOT A REAL MATTER]

Observed — readable in the artefacts. Supported — inferred from two or more independent artefact classes that agree. Consistent with — not contradicted, not established; other explanations named. Not established — the material does not answer it, and this one is used as freely as the rest.

Limitations

  1. Timestamps are as recorded by the source system. Where artefacts disagree, the disagreement is reported.
  2. The examination reaches what was collected; the manifest lists what was not.
  3. Nothing predates the earliest surviving log entry, and read receipts were unavailable at that licence tier.
  4. The removable device was not produced.
  5. No attribution to a person. A device is not a person.
  6. Recovery of deleted data depends on medium, filesystem and encryption. Failed attempts are recorded, not omitted.
  7. If further material arrives, findings may change, and I will say so in writing.

What digital evidence cannot establish, in full, is at /limits.
