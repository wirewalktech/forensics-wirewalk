What I will not do

Each one is a term in the engagement letter.

I will not

  1. Take a fee that moves with what I find, the outcome, or which side retains me.
  2. Take or pay a referral fee, take a vendor commission, or sell the remediation I was paid to investigate.
  3. Write to a conclusion supplied in advance.
  4. Say who was at the keyboard from device evidence alone. A device is not a person.
  5. Bill an hour above the ceiling you authorised in writing.
  6. Image a whole disk where a targeted collection answers it, or bill for volume I expanded.
  7. Stay quiet when culling would take your bill from five figures to four.
  8. Touch a device, mailbox or account without documented authority — a spouse's phone included.
  9. Take a matter I cannot finish by your deadline, or one needing a licence I lack.
  10. Act for both sides, or as a neutral where I have advised either side.
  11. Promise a finding, an unlocked handset, or that anything I produce will be admitted.
