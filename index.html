What I will not do

Each is a term in the engagement letter.

I will not

  1. Take a fee that moves with what I find, the outcome, or which side retains me.
  2. Take or pay a referral fee, take a vendor commission, or sell the remediation I investigated.
  3. Write to a conclusion supplied in advance, or report an inference as a finding.
  4. Say who was at the keyboard from device evidence alone. A device is not a person.
  5. Opine that a party intended to destroy evidence. Intent is the court's finding.
  6. Bill above your written ceiling, or run an uncapped password or extraction attempt.
  7. Carry an overage or surge rate; hours past a standby credit bill at the retained rate.
  8. Deduct card processing fees from a balance I am returning.
  9. Image a whole disk where a targeted collection answers it, or bill for volume I expanded.
  10. Stay quiet when culling would take your bill from five figures to four.
  11. Touch a device, mailbox or account without documented authority — a spouse's phone included.
  12. Start work, hold a date or take a file before conflicts clear and the letter is signed.
  13. Take a matter I cannot finish by your deadline, or one needing a license I lack.
  14. Act for both sides, or as a neutral where I have advised either side.
  15. Promise a finding, a recovered file, an unlocked handset, or that anything I produce will be admitted.
