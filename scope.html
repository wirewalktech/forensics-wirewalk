How the prices work

  • FIXED — a stated price for a stated unit. It buys an artefact: an image, an extraction, a copy, a certificate. Not a conclusion drawn from it.
  • HOURLY · CEILING — the rate on the rate schedule, plus a written stop-point. No hour above the ceiling is billed. At 80% of it I stop and report, and work resumes only on a written increase.
  • TRIAGE · SCOPED — a fixed first block, credited in full against whatever follows it. It sometimes ends in advice to stop.
  • FIXED UNIT — the unit price is published, the total is not, because the quantity is not mine to know until the work is done.

The rate is the same whichever side retains me, it is fixed before I look at anything, and it does not move with what I find.

Position. TAKE — I do the work. REFER OUT — real work I can specify and read the output of, but do not perform here; I name someone who does and take no fee in either direction. DECLINE — I do not do it, and would not arrange it.

Cloud and SaaS

You cannot write-block a cloud account, so soundness comes from method: tool and version recorded, the export hashed at the earliest point I can reach, and the authority relied on plus the tenant's retention and licence tier written down at collection. A log that was never switched on cannot be recovered afterwards.

What is never charged

No line here carries a minimum-hours charge. The only minimums on this site are the testimony ones, and the reason is printed beside them. There is no typical-hours figure either; you get the opening authorisation I would propose for your matter.

Three things are free: the scoping and preservation call, up to 45 minutes, with a written preservation instruction if one is needed; the call that ends with me saying the data cannot answer your question; and the call that ends with me saying you do not need a forensic examiner at all.

Tools are named on this site as categories, not as licences held; the tool and version actually used goes into the notes and the report. Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a licence, that is identified at scoping and written into the engagement letter — either I associate an examiner who holds it, or I decline. My method is written against ISO/IEC 27037:2012 and NIST SP 800-86: guidance I work to, not accreditation I hold. This is not an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory, and no line here promises a finding, a recovered file, an unlocked handset, or admission.
