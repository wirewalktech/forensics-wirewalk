How the prices work

FIXED buys a stated artefact. HOURLY · CEILING is the published rate against a written stop-point: nothing above it is billed, and at 80% I stop and report. TRIAGE is a fixed first block, credited against what follows. FIXED UNIT publishes the unit, not the total.

Everything below I TAKE. Refer-outs and declines are listed last.

What is never charged

No minimum-hours charge here; the only minimums are for testimony.

Free: the scoping and preservation call, up to 45 minutes; the call where the data cannot answer your question; the call where you do not need me.

Where a matter requires a certified examiner, an accredited laboratory or a licence, that is stated in the engagement letter, or I decline. Method follows ISO/IEC 27037:2012 and NIST SP 800-86 — guidance, not accreditation. Not an ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory; nothing here promises a finding.
